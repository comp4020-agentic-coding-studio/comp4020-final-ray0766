# Crit 10 reflection

> Based on my Chinese response; translated and edited into English with AI assistance.

The breakthrough that moved the work forward was making loading smoother. Little Worlds uses real-time 3D graphics, so getting into the world is part of the experience, not just a step before playing. This week's work addressed arrival preparation and rendering load, and added a clearer explanation of what to try if loading is slow or fails. My classmates also played, and what I observed was that the website ran normally. That is the extent of the result I reported; it does not establish that every action was traced in the logs or that the app has no remaining problems.

This work changed how I want to start developing with AI. I want to have a good idea first, then prepare a sample or reference for the AI to learn from and align with, and only then begin implementation. I do not want to jump straight into making something before that direction is clear. A reference gives the idea something concrete to point to, instead of leaving the AI to guess what I mean. The change I want in my own process is therefore about preparation as well as execution: deciding what I am trying to make and giving the AI a useful example before asking it to build.
