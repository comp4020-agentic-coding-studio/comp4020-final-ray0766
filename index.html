little wanderer.

A little world is waiting for your first idea. Choose a suit, then follow the blue-lit street to STARPORT / 01. Press E near its boarding gate.

Your coat, home and creations are saved for this browser identity. A SMALL FIELD GUIDE

Take your time.

Walk with WASD or the arrow keys. You can also click the ground to walk there, or drag the thumbstick on a touch screen. In Sunseed Harbour, drag the open scene to look around and up at the buildings. Use Planet view to see a wider view.

Walk along the blue-lit street to the STARPORT building. Near the boarding terminal, press E or tap Board your ship. On other planets, your temporary return beacon and parked ship stay at the landing point. Use Pause → Find my ship for an optional direction marker. Cyan foot rings mark other visitors on this planet. The Atlas (M) groups finite destinations into Harbour Belt, Lantern Reach and Outer Drift. In space, hold W to thrust, A / D to turn, ↑ / ↓ to climb or dive, and S to brake. On a phone, use the steering stick and hold Thrust or Brake. Mark a bearing in the Atlas, fly within 20 m of its centre and slow below 6 m/s to land. Open Pause on a blank planet to choose Make this my planet, then explicitly enter Build on my planet. In Build mode, choose an object, tap the ground, and save it. Tap an existing object to move, rotate or remove it. Only its owner can edit.

The camera follows you around the sphere. Leave Build mode to walk to a new area. Sunseed Harbour also has an optional delivery: find Mica, press E to collect, then bring it to Sol.

Your browser identity owns your home. Clearing cookies loses access; there is no account recovery yet.
