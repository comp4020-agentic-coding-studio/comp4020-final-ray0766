 MAKE YOURSELF AT HOME

Good morning,
little wanderer.

A little world is waiting for your first idea. Choose a coat, then open the star map.

Your coat, home and creations are saved for this browser identity. A SMALL FIELD GUIDE

Take your time.

Walk with WASD or the arrow keys. You can also click the ground to walk there, or drag the thumbstick on a touch screen. Use Planet view to see a wider view.

Open the Star map to visit any planet. On a blank planet, choose Make this my planet. In Build mode, choose an object, tap the ground, and save it. Tap an existing object to move, rotate or remove it. Only its owner can edit.

The camera follows you around the sphere. Leave Build mode to walk to a new area. Sunseed Harbour also has an optional delivery: find Mica, press E to collect, then bring it to Sol.

Your browser identity owns your home. Clearing cookies loses access; there is no account recovery yet.
