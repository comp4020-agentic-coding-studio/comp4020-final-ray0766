 A SMALL FIELD GUIDE

Take your time.

Walk with WASD or the arrow keys. Hold Shift to run. Click the scene or Click to look, then move the mouse to look around without holding a button. Press Esc to release the cursor. Press Tab in the scene to open Pause. In menus, Tab and Shift+Tab move focus; use Close or Resume to return. W moves toward the view, S walks backward, and A / D step sideways. Menus and editors release the mouse; click again to resume looking. On touch screens, drag the scene to look, tap the ground to walk, or use the thumbstick. If mouse capture is unavailable, drag to look. Use Planet view to see a wider view.

Choose a destination. Takeoff, flight and landing are automatic. Drag to look around. Click a planet to confirm a new destination. Skip waits for preparation; Cancel returns to departure.

The camera follows you around the sphere. Leave Build mode to walk to a new area. Sunseed Harbour also has an optional delivery: find Mica, press E to collect, then bring it to Sol.

Your browser identity owns your home. Clearing cookies loses access; there is no account recovery yet.
